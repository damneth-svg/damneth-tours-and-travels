01

Ancient kingdoms

Cultural Triangle

Rock fortresses, cave temples and ruined royal cities sit close together on the dry central plains. Most travellers stay near Sigiriya and take short drives out each day, then finish in Kandy on the way to the hills.

  • Best January to September
  • Allow 2 to 4 days
UNESCO World Heritage

Sigiriya

The 5th-century rock fortress rises straight out of the jungle, with painted frescoes and giant lion’s paws halfway up. It’s the island’s most famous climb, and worth every step. We also run Sigiriya as a day tour.

  • Climb at opening time, before the heat and the crowds
  • Watch the sunrise over Sigiriya from nearby Pidurangala Rock
  • An easy base for Dambulla, Polonnaruwa and Minneriya

Ideal stay 2 nights

UNESCO World Heritage

Dambulla

Five cave shrines carved into a granite outcrop, filled with painted ceilings and more than 150 Buddha statues. It sits on the road to Kandy, so it fits neatly into a travel day.

  • Cave paintings that cover every surface
  • Wide views over the plains from the terrace
  • Cover shoulders and knees, and leave shoes at the gate

Ideal stay Half a day

UNESCO World Heritage

Polonnaruwa

A medieval royal capital of palaces, temples and the calm, rock-cut Buddhas of Gal Vihara, spread across a leafy, walkable site.

  • The Gal Vihara rock carvings
  • Explore the ruins by bicycle
  • Pairs well with an afternoon safari at Minneriya

Ideal stay 1 day

UNESCO World Heritage

Anuradhapura

Sri Lanka’s first great capital, with vast white dagobas and a sacred fig tree that is one of the oldest known trees in the world. Quieter and more spread out than Polonnaruwa.

  • The huge white Ruwanwelisaya dagoba
  • The sacred Sri Maha Bodhi tree
  • Best on a longer trip of 10 days or more

Ideal stay 1 day

UNESCO World Heritage

Kandy

The old hill capital wraps around a lake and is home to the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic. It’s the gateway between the ancient cities and tea country.

  • Evening puja (prayers) at the Temple of the Tooth
  • The Royal Botanic Gardens at Peradeniya
  • We also run a Kandy cultural day tour

Ideal stay 1 to 2 nights

02

Tea and cool air

Hill Country

Cool air, tea on every slope, waterfalls and the famous blue train. The roads up here are narrow and winding, which is exactly where a careful, unhurried driver makes all the difference.

  • Clearest views January to April
  • Allow 3 to 4 days

Ella

A laid-back hill village with big views in every direction. Days here are for short hikes, long breakfasts and watching the train cross the Nine Arches Bridge.

  • An easy sunrise walk up Little Adam’s Peak
  • See a train cross the Nine Arches Bridge
  • Arrive on the scenic train from Kandy or Nanu Oya

Ideal stay 2 nights

Nuwara Eliya

Known as “Little England” for its colonial bungalows and cool climate, this is the heart of tea country.

  • Tour a working tea factory and taste fresh tea
  • Stroll or take a boat on Gregory Lake
  • Bring a warm layer for the evenings

Ideal stay 1 night

UNESCO World Heritage

Horton Plains

A high, windswept plateau of grassland and cloud forest that ends at a sheer drop called World’s End.

  • The World’s End loop walk, about 9 km
  • Start at dawn, before the mist rolls in
  • Baker’s Falls along the way

Ideal stay Half a day

03

Beaches and a fort town

Southern Coast

Palm-lined bays, the old Dutch fort at Galle and whale boats heading out at dawn. The south coast is where most trips slow down for a few days on the sand.

  • Best December to April
  • Allow 3 to 5 days
UNESCO World Heritage

Galle

A walled 17th-century fort town of cobbled lanes, cafés and small shops, wrapped in ramparts above the sea.

  • Walk the ramparts at sunset
  • The lighthouse, churches and old Dutch streets
  • An easy expressway drive back to the airport

Ideal stay 1 to 2 nights

Mirissa

A curve of golden sand with a relaxed beach-town feel, and the best-known place on the island to go whale watching.

  • Whale-watching boats, roughly November to April
  • Sunset at Coconut Tree Hill
  • Calm swimming in the bay in season

Ideal stay 2 nights

Weligama

A wide, sandy bay with soft, rolling waves that make it one of the best places in Sri Lanka to learn to surf.

  • Beginner-friendly waves and surf schools on the beach
  • See the stilt fishermen near Koggala
  • Ask us about surf trip transfers

Ideal stay 2 to 3 nights

04

The other monsoon

East Coast

When the rains reach the south and west, the east coast comes into its own: calm turquoise water, coral reefs and long, quiet beaches.

  • Best May to September
  • Allow 3 to 4 days

Trincomalee

A deep natural harbour with clifftop temples, and white-sand beaches at Nilaveli and Uppuveli just up the coast.

  • Snorkel among reef fish at Pigeon Island
  • Koneswaram Temple on Swami Rock
  • Beach days at Nilaveli and Uppuveli

Ideal stay 2 nights

Arugam Bay

A surf town on the far south-east coast, famous for its long point break and easy-going village life.

  • A world-famous point break for surfers
  • Lagoon and wildlife trips towards Kumana
  • Best on a 10 to 14-day trip

Ideal stay 3 nights

Pasikudah

A shallow, sheltered bay where you can wade a long way out in warm, calm water. Lovely for families.

  • Shallow water, great for children
  • Glassy, calm sea in season
  • We can help you book a hotel here

Ideal stay 2 nights

05

Leopards and elephants

Wildlife Parks

Leopards, elephants, crocodiles and hundreds of bird species. Jeep safaris run early in the morning and late in the afternoon, when the animals are most active.

  • Good most of the year
  • Allow 1 day per park

Yala

Sri Lanka’s best-known national park, famous for its leopards, with elephants, sloth bears and crocodiles too.

  • Early-morning jeep safaris for the best sightings
  • The park often closes for a few weeks around September
  • We arrange Yala safari day tours

Ideal stay 1 night nearby

Udawalawe

Open grassland around a large reservoir, and one of the most reliable places on the island to see wild elephants.

  • Herds of elephants all year round
  • The Elephant Transit Home at feeding time
  • An easy stop on the way to the south coast

Ideal stay Half a day

Minneriya

In the dry season, hundreds of elephants gather on the grassy shores of the ancient Minneriya reservoir.

  • “The Gathering”, roughly July to October
  • Nearby Kaudulla when the herds move on
  • Close to Sigiriya and Polonnaruwa

Ideal stay Half a day
