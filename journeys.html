7-Day Classic

The island’s highlights in one week: the rock fortress at Sigiriya, Kandy’s sacred temple, the train through tea country, a leopard safari in Yala and a last walk around Galle Fort.

The route

  1. Airport
  2. Sigiriya
  3. Kandy
  4. Nuwara Eliya
  5. Ella
  6. Yala
  7. Galle
  • AC private vehicle
  • English-speaking driver
  • All tolls & fuel included
  • Hotel booking assistance
Day-by-day plan for the 7-Day Classic Show 7 daysHide
  1. Day1

    Day 1: Airport to Sigiriya

    Your driver meets you at arrivals at Bandaranaike International Airport. A relaxed drive north to the Cultural Triangle, stopping for lunch on the way.

    Overnight: Sigiriya

  2. Day2

    Day 2: Sigiriya and Dambulla

    Climb Sigiriya Rock at opening time, before the heat. After lunch, visit the painted cave temples at Dambulla.

    Overnight: Sigiriya

  3. Day3

    Day 3: Into the hills to Kandy

    Drive up to Kandy with a stop at a spice garden. In the evening, join the puja at the Temple of the Tooth.

    Overnight: Kandy

  4. Day4

    Day 4: Tea country

    A winding climb past tea estates and waterfalls, with a tea factory visit and tasting. Evening in cool Nuwara Eliya.

    Overnight: Nuwara Eliya

  5. Day5

    Day 5: The train to Ella

    Ride the scenic train from Nanu Oya to Ella. Your driver goes ahead by road with your bags and meets you there.

    Overnight: Ella

  6. Day6

    Day 6: Ella to Yala

    An easy sunrise walk up Little Adam’s Peak, then down to the lowlands for an afternoon jeep safari in Yala.

    Overnight: Near Yala

  7. Day7

    Day 7: Galle and the airport

    West along the coast to Galle Fort for a walk on the ramparts, then the expressway back to the airport for your flight.

    Airport drop-off

Day-by-day plan for the 10-Day Island Highlights Show 10 daysHide
  1. Day1

    Day 1: Airport to Sigiriya

    Your driver meets you at arrivals at Bandaranaike International Airport and takes you north to the Cultural Triangle.

    Overnight: Sigiriya

  2. Day2

    Day 2: Sigiriya Rock

    Climb Sigiriya at opening time, or Pidurangala for the view of it. A free afternoon by the pool or on a village walk.

    Overnight: Sigiriya

  3. Day3

    Day 3: Polonnaruwa and Minneriya

    Cycle around the ancient city of Polonnaruwa, then an afternoon jeep safari to see elephants at Minneriya or nearby Kaudulla.

    Overnight: Sigiriya

  4. Day4

    Day 4: Dambulla to Kandy

    Visit the Dambulla cave temples on the way into the hills, with a stop at a spice garden. Evening puja at the Temple of the Tooth.

    Overnight: Kandy

  5. Day5

    Day 5: A slow day in Kandy

    The Royal Botanic Gardens at Peradeniya, a walk around the lake and a Kandyan dance show in the evening.

    Overnight: Kandy

  6. Day6

    Day 6: Tea country

    Up through tea estates and past waterfalls to Nuwara Eliya, with a tea factory visit along the way.

    Overnight: Nuwara Eliya

  7. Day7

    Day 7: The train to Ella

    Ride the scenic train to Ella while your driver brings your bags by road. Afternoon at the Nine Arches Bridge.

    Overnight: Ella

  8. Day8

    Day 8: Ella to Yala

    Little Adam’s Peak at sunrise, then down to Yala for an afternoon jeep safari.

    Overnight: Near Yala

  9. Day9

    Day 9: To the south coast

    Along the coast to Mirissa for an afternoon on the beach and sunset at Coconut Tree Hill.

    Overnight: Mirissa

  10. Day10

    Day 10: Galle and the airport

    Whale watching if it’s in season, or a slow morning. Then Galle Fort and the expressway to the airport.

    Airport drop-off

The whole island

14-Day Ultimate

Two weeks to see it all: the sacred northern cities, the east coast beaches, the highlands, a safari and the south. Best from May to September, when the east coast is calm.

The route

  1. Airport
  2. Negombo
  3. Anuradhapura
  4. Sigiriya
  5. Trincomalee
  6. Kandy
  7. Ella
  8. Yala
  9. Galle
  • AC private vehicle
  • English-speaking driver
  • All tolls & fuel included
  • Hotel booking assistance
Day-by-day plan for the 14-Day Ultimate Show 14 daysHide
  1. Day1

    Day 1: Airport to Negombo

    Your driver meets you at arrivals. A short drive to Negombo for a gentle first evening by the sea.

    Overnight: Negombo

  2. Day2

    Day 2: Anuradhapura

    North to Sri Lanka’s first great capital: giant white dagobas and the sacred Sri Maha Bodhi tree.

    Overnight: Anuradhapura

  3. Day3

    Day 3: Mihintale to Sigiriya

    Climb the stone steps at Mihintale, where Buddhism is said to have arrived in Sri Lanka, then on to Sigiriya.

    Overnight: Sigiriya

  4. Day4

    Day 4: Sigiriya and Dambulla

    Sigiriya Rock at opening time, then the painted cave temples at Dambulla.

    Overnight: Sigiriya

  5. Day5

    Day 5: Polonnaruwa and Minneriya

    Cycle the ruins of Polonnaruwa, then an afternoon elephant safari at Minneriya or Kaudulla.

    Overnight: Sigiriya

  6. Day6

    Day 6: East to Trincomalee

    Across to the east coast for an afternoon on the white sand at Nilaveli or Uppuveli.

    Overnight: Trincomalee

  7. Day7

    Day 7: Pigeon Island

    Snorkel among reef fish at Pigeon Island, then visit Koneswaram Temple on its clifftop.

    Overnight: Trincomalee

  8. Day8

    Day 8: Back into the hills

    A long, scenic drive west and up into the hills to Kandy, in time for the evening puja.

    Overnight: Kandy

  9. Day9

    Day 9: Tea country

    Up through tea estates and waterfalls to Nuwara Eliya, with a tea factory visit.

    Overnight: Nuwara Eliya

  10. Day10

    Day 10: Horton Plains to Ella

    An early walk to World’s End on Horton Plains, then on to Ella.

    Overnight: Ella

  11. Day11

    Day 11: A free day in Ella

    Little Adam’s Peak, the Nine Arches Bridge and Ella’s cafés, at your own pace.

    Overnight: Ella

  12. Day12

    Day 12: Yala

    Down to the lowlands for an afternoon jeep safari in Yala.

    Overnight: Near Yala

  13. Day13

    Day 13: The south coast

    West along the coast to Mirissa for an afternoon on the beach.

    Overnight: Mirissa

  14. Day14

    Day 14: Galle and the airport

    A morning in Galle Fort, then the expressway to the airport for your flight.

    Airport drop-off
